 Key reasoning

 The paths must satisfy both the one-way-road directions and the restriction against sharing a directed road.
 Merely finding a shortest path for each team independently is insufficient: A→B→D→E (9 minutes) and
 B→D→F (8 minutes) both use B→D, so that pair is invalid.

 Q6 — 9 minutes

 Team 1 cannot reach E in less than 9 minutes: two shortest routes are A→B→D→E = 3+3+3 = 9 and
 A→C→D→E = 4+2+3 = 9. Team 2 cannot reach F in less than 8 minutes: B→D→F = 3+5 = 8.

 The combination A→C→D→E (9) and B→D→F (8) uses no common directed road, so both finish by minute 9.
 The lower bound is achieved.
